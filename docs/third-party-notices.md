## Proton

Roll Guardian includes Proton v7.1.5, a JavaScript particle animation engine.

- Project: https://github.com/drawcall/Proton
- License: MIT
- Vendored browser bundle: `dist/vendor/proton.web.min.js`
- Distributed license: `dist/vendor/LICENSE.proton.txt`

The Roll Guardian adapter uses Proton emitters, rates, spans, particle lifetimes, velocities, colors, alpha and scale transitions, drift, gravity, cyclone motion, easing, and its custom renderer. The effect configurations are adapted from Proton’s flame, sparks, snow, random-drift, and custom-renderer examples.
